AdGauge - AdMob Dashboard

About

Your AdMob revenue, live on your phone.

AdGauge shows AdMob publishers their estimated earnings, apps and ad units without opening the AdMob console. It pulls live revenue data from the AdMob API for the Google account you connect, on both Android and iOS.

Features

  • Estimated earnings for today, yesterday, this month and last month
  • Requests, impressions, match rate, eCPM and show rate with period comparisons
  • Flexible date ranges, revenue trend chart and top ad units
  • Per-app breakdowns by ad format, country and platform
  • Google Sign-In with read-only AdMob access
