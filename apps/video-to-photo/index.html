Video To Photo - Frame Grabber

About

Capture frames from any video and save them as high-quality photos.

Pick a video, then grab a single frame with Quick Capture or extract many frames at once with Time Interval. Every photo keeps the same resolution as the video and can be saved to your Gallery in its own album.

Features

  • Quick Capture and Time Interval extraction modes
  • Flexible rates, from 1 frame per minute to several frames per second
  • Save frames to your phone Gallery with custom albums
  • Pick videos from the built-in list or your phone storage
  • Review and delete frames, including multiple at once
