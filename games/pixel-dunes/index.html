Pixel Dunes

About

A relaxing pixel-art color sort puzzle.

Pour layers of pixels, sand and crystal between tubes until each tube holds a single color. Handcrafted pixel art, no timers, and free to play.

Features

  • One-tap sorting: tap a tube, then its destination
  • Every level verified solvable by a built-in solver
  • Unlimited undo, restart and hints
  • Three-star scoring based on par moves
  • Play offline, anytime
